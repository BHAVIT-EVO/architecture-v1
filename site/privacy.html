Evo / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This is the formal version; Privacy and trust says the same things in plain language.

1. What Evo processes

Evo reads the title and source of the window you are in, and the document it belongs to, so it can offer that work back to you later. If you grant folder, app or site access, Evo processes only what you allowed.

2. Where it is processed

On your Mac. Speech recognition, wake-word detection and search run locally. Evo does not transmit audio, documents, window titles or search queries to us or to any third party.

3. What we collect

Nothing by default. Evo has no account system, no analytics and no advertising identifiers. Optional anonymous analytics are off unless you turn them on, and the event list is published in the changelog and the guide. Analytics never include content.

4. Crash reports and problem reports

Crash reporting is opt-in. When you use Report a problem, Evo shows the exact payload — app version, macOS version, microphone state, recent activity lines — and sends nothing until you approve it. You may copy it instead and send it yourself.

5. Data you create

Your works, settings, permissions and activity live in Evo's store on this Mac. You can export them as JSON, or delete everything from Activity → Data. Deleting is immediate and cannot be undone.

6. Retention

You choose how long history is kept in Settings → Privacy and Trust (forever, one year, or 90 days). Activity is capped at the most recent 100 entries.

7. Your rights

Because the data is on your machine, access, correction, export and deletion are all available in the app. If you believe we hold personal data about you, write to hello@evo.app.

8. Children

Evo is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone by default.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the change is listed in the changelog.

10. Contact

Contact us or email hello@evo.app.
