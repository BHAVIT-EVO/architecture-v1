0.1.0 — first release

The first version of Evo. What is in it:

  • Home: the scene, the list view, search with ⌘K, rename, merge, hide and undo.
  • The notch: listening, thinking, acting, done, needs you, error and paused.
  • Intro and onboarding, replayable from Help.
  • Settings, Activity with undo, permissions with three modes, export and delete.
  • Help, Diagnostics, the tray menu, and every state: loading, empty, error, offline, permission denied.
  • Everything on this Mac. No account, no analytics, no uploads.

Known gaps

  • Memory and Autopilot are not in this release. They are named on the home page so you know what is missing.
  • The menu-bar menu is macOS-only; the web preview shows a stand-in.
