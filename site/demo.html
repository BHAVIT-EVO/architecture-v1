The verifier

What is real here. The engine is real: 16 Rust crates, deterministic, offline, no HTTP client, no model in the decision path. The gate below is the real assertion set from crates/evo-threads — it fails the build if two bodies of work ever fuse.

What is not. This page runs a synthetic corpus so that publishing it does not publish anyone's browsing history. The two buttons demonstrate the gate: they simulate a fusion and show the verifier rejecting it. They do not write to the engine.

Why so few claimed. Two of eight works are claimed; six are refused. That is the design, not a defect — a wrong merge is irreversible and a refusal costs nothing. A boundary that guesses is not a boundary.
