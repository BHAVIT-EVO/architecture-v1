The first build is not attached yet. The buttons take you to the setup page so you can see what happens next; when the signed build ships, the same links carry it.

After you download

  1. Open the DMG and drag Evo to Applications.
  2. Open Evo. macOS asks for the microphone, so Evo can hear “Hey Evo”.
  3. Say “Hey Evo”. The notch opens and Evo is listening.

Stuck on any of it? The guide covers each step, and contact reaches a person.

What it needs

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later.
  • A microphone, built in or connected. Evo works without one if you type or press ⌘K.
  • No account, no internet connection.
