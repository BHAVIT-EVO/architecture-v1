Evo / Privacy and trust

Privacy and trust

Plain language, no hedging. If a claim here is ever untrue, it is a bug and we want to hear about it.

What Evo can see

The window you are in, and the document it belongs to. That is how it knows where you left off.

With your permission it can also see folders, apps and sites — you choose which, and you can change your mind in Settings → Privacy and Trust.

What Evo never does

  • No screen recording. Not a frame, not a screenshot.
  • No keystroke log.
  • No audio or text leaving this Mac.
  • No account, and nothing to sync.
  • No analytics unless you turn them on, and never with content.

Where your work lives

On this Mac, in Evo's own store. You can export it as JSON, or delete everything from Activity → Data. Deleting removes the works, the settings, the permissions and the activity log.

How you check it

Activity lists every action with what, when and why, and offers Undo where it can. Nothing Evo does is invisible.

Diagnostics shows the microphone, permissions, voice engine and storage. The microphone is only open while Evo is listening; the listening chip in the top bar tells you which.

Pause

Pause everything stops capture, listening and every action. It sits at the top of Activity, in Settings, and in the menu bar.

Reports

When you report a problem, Evo shows the exact text that would be sent — app version, macOS, the microphone state, and the last few activity lines — and waits for your approval. You can copy it and send it yourself instead.

Questions

Ask at Contact. The Privacy Policy is the formal version of this page.
