Guide

Everything Evo does, in the order you'll need it. Every page here works without JavaScript.

First run

Open Evo. The intro plays once — about twenty seconds, skippable with any key. Then four short setup steps: what Evo is, the microphone, window access, and saying “Hey Evo”.

Each permission is asked on its own, with why it's needed and what happens if you say no. Setup remembers where you stopped.

Getting back to work

Your recent work sits on the path, newest nearest. Click an object, or use the arrow keys and Enter. List view is the same set as rows, for the keyboard.

Each work's menu offers Rename, Merge with…, This isn't work and Hide from Home. Hiding is reversible from Activity.

Voice

Say “Hey Evo”, then what you want: “open the Q3 roadmap”, “find the invoice”, “what was I doing”. The notch shows a live transcript as you speak; nothing is sent anywhere.

No microphone? Type the same words, or press ⌘K.

Search

⌘K opens search over your work. Type a few letters, arrow to a result, press Enter. Evo brings the window forward.

Activity and permissions

Activity lists what Evo did, when and why, with Undo where an action can be reversed. Permissions has three modes: ask every time, ask for risky actions, or act within a scope you set for folders, apps and sites.

Pause everything sits at the top of Activity, in Settings, and in the menu bar. Export or delete everything from the Data tab.

Troubleshooting

It didn't hear me

Open Diagnostics and run the microphone test. If it hears nothing, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and the input device.

The notch doesn't open

Evo may be paused. Resume from the tray, then say “Hey Evo” again. The hotkey still works while the voice engine is unavailable.

Something went wrong

Diagnostics shows the microphone, permissions, voice engine and storage. If you need to report it, the report shows exactly what would be sent, and waits for your approval.

Keyboard

  • ⌘K — search your work
  • ← → — move the selection, Enter opens
  • S — switch Scene and List
  • ⌥Space — listen now
  • ⌥⌘P — pause everything
  • ? — open Help
