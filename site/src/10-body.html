Field note 001 31.10.2026 — Gurugram Ship Happens / AWS × ADIGPS

Every agent is told where its work begins. Evo derives it.

The boundary layer for agents: where one body of work ends and the next begins — derived on the device, without a model, so an agent can act on a real machine without a human labelling every folder first.

02Reconstructionthe part
nobody bills

You spend the firstten minutes becomingyourself again.

Not recalling the task. Rebuilding the shape of it — which of the forty-seven open windows mattered, which tab was the thread, which document was only ever open because something was cited in it.

windows open47
Forty-seven surfaces. One of them is the job.The machine keeps all of them. It does not keep the shape — what was made, what you returned to, what you only glanced at.
So you rebuild it by hand, every time.A hundred and sixty-three times a day, in the background, for free.
Evo derives the shape instead.From the making, the returning and the glancing. One boundary, drawn from evidence, on the device.
03The category errorbytes are
not meaning

A folder is not a boundary.

It is a decision you made before the work existed — a name, a menu, a filing cabinet. Real work does not arrive like that. And two people can hold identical bytes for entirely different reasons.

The boundary is not in the bytes. It is in how the work arrived: what you made, what you returned to, and what you merely glanced at. That distinction is the entire engine — and it is why an agent cannot read it out of the file system, no matter how long its context window.

A boundary that guesses is not a boundary. The design constraint the whole engine is built around
04The enginedeterministic
fold

Every event, in order.Eight bodies of work out.Two it will defend.

No model. No embeddings. No sampling. A fold over the log that produces the same boundary every time it runs — because a boundary that changes tomorrow is not something you can give an agent permission to act on.

Every event, in order.Two days on one machine: what was typed, what was focused, what was visited and for how long.
Attention becomes episodes. Episodes become works.Points are pulled to the bodies of work they earned — not the ones they resemble.

07Jurisdictionnot learning —
legislation

It does not learn.The person legislates.

Six kinds of declaration exist in the engine. Each one is permanent, recorded in the log, and honoured on every replay. There is no feedback loop that can quietly undo a person's decision.

Derived

The engine placed it, from evidence it witnessed.

Declared

The person overrode or confirmed it, and the log holds that.

Refused

The engine declined to place it at all.

DeclarationThe person saysWhat the engine does
SameWork“These belong together”The only merge path in the system.
SeparateWork“These are separate”A standing prohibition. Inference can never rejoin them — not by score, not by evidence, not ever.
NotMine“This is not my work”Permanent quarantine. Never evidence again.
Classified“This surface is of a class”Gates founding and titling only. Keeps the attention it earned.
WorkDismissed“Don’t show this work”Evidence retained; hidden from the rack.
MemberStateA lifecycle correctionApplies to that one member.
These are separate works; inference will never put them together. assign.rs — recorded in the log, honoured on every replay

The engine implements all six and honours them on replay. The service exposes no endpoint for them yet — today a declaration is made by replaying a log, not by calling an API. That is the next thing to build, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.

10Colophonwhat is real
and what is not

We would rather you trusttwo works than be impressedby eight.

Real

  • 16 Rust crates. A deterministic fold over an event log.
  • The gate: no fusion, no leisure anchor, determinism. It fails the build.
  • 10,392 real events. 164 resources labelled by hand.
  • An HTTP service and an MCP server, both local.
  • Local-first enforced by a test that walks every manifest.

Simulated

  • The gate buttons on this page. They show the verifier tripping; they do not write to the engine.
  • This site's corpus. Synthetic, eight invented works.
  • The concept film. A motion study, not a screen recording.

Not yet

  • An endpoint for declarations. The engine honours them; the service cannot be asked for them.
  • Ten people outside the company running it for thirty days.
  • Cross-device sync. The film is what it looks like when it lands.

One person, one machine, two days. Every number on this page is either in the corpus above, in the repository, or in a named published study. Where a figure could not be traced to a primary source — the widely repeated 78% MCP figure, for instance — we left it out rather than repeat it.

Every agent shipping right now has memory. None of them have jurisdiction.
