About

Evo helps people get back into their work. That is the whole idea, and everything else is in service of it.

Why it exists

Every day you open dozens of things, and then you lose your place. Not because you forgot the work, but because the machine did. Tabs close, windows hide behind windows, and the thread goes with them.

Evo keeps that thread. Not as a list to maintain, but as a place you return to: a clearing at dusk, your recent work on the path, newest nearest.

How it's built

A native Mac app, mostly in Rust, with a small pixel-art world drawn on a canvas and a quiet interface around it. Voice and search run on the device. There is no server holding your work.

What we won't do

  • No feed, no streaks, no notifications asking you back.
  • No account, and nothing to sync.
  • No claims we can't show you in Activity or Diagnostics.
  • No invented numbers on this website. If something isn't shipped, it says so.

Free

Evo is free to use. If that changes, it will be said plainly here and in the app, before anything is charged.
