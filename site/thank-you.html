Three steps to your first “Hey Evo”.

Roughly two minutes. You can skip the microphone and type instead at any point.

1

Install it

Open the DMG, drag Evo into Applications, then open it. The first launch is from Finder, not from this page.

2

Let it hear you

macOS asks for the microphone. Allow it. If you'd rather not, type “Hey Evo” into the onboarding card instead.

3

Say it

Say “Hey Evo” out loud. The notch opens, the waveform moves, and Evo says what it heard.

Then what

Open a couple of documents and close them. They appear on the path as objects, newest nearest. Press ⌘K to search them; press ⌘K again to search your work anywhere.

Nothing heard? The guide has the microphone fix, the permission reset, and what to do if the voice engine fails. Still stuck, tell us — the report shows exactly what would be sent, and you approve it first.
