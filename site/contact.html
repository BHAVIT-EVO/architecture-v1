Contact

A person reads this. Say what happened, what you expected, and what you tried.

Email

hello@evo.app

Ideas and opinions are welcome. Bug reports get priority; the fastest route is the report button in Help, which shows you exactly what would be sent before anything leaves the app.

What to include

  • What you did, in order.
  • What happened, and what you expected instead.
  • Your macOS version, and whether the Mac is Apple silicon or Intel.
  • Whether voice or the hotkey still worked.

Before you write

Many answers are on the guide: the microphone fix, the permission reset, and what to do when the voice engine fails.

Privacy questions are answered in plain language on Privacy and trust; the formal version is the Privacy Policy.

Reports

Nothing is sent from Evo without your approval. The preview contains the app version, your macOS version, the microphone state, and the last few activity lines — never your documents, your words, or audio.
