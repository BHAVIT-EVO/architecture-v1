# Evo — Logo Design Brief

## What Evo is
A local-first *continuity system* for a person's work on their Mac. It witnesses
what you actually did — files, windows, pages — and rebuilds the environment a
thought belongs in, so you continue instead of reconstructing.

> "You should only have to remember **what** you want to do.
> Never **how** to get back to doing it."
> — docs/product/PRODUCT.md

## The product's own words (the raw material)
- "Human thought is continuous. The digital world is not." — Constitution, Preamble
- "once a thought has begun, it never has to begin again"
- "Continuity Is The Product." — Article IV
- "A system that is occasionally silent is preferable to one that is confidently wrong." — Article III
- "Reality must never be altered to preserve an interpretation." — Article II
- "We will measure our success not by how much Evo knows, but by how little our users must remember." — Founder's Oath
- "It should feel like the interruption never happened." — PRODUCT.md

## Audience
A solo builder running many parallel works. Opens Evo to decide *"what do I continue?"*
Not a team tool, not a chatbot, not a dashboard.

## Brand adjectives
1. **Continuous** — never starts over; one thread
2. **Quiet** — watches, does not perform
3. **Precise** — evidence, not guesses
4. **Warm** — caramel on charcoal, a studio at night, not cold SaaS
5. **Disciplined** — "The Product Must Remain Smaller Than The Vision"

## Constraints
- Must survive **16 px**, one colour, reversed, embroidery, wallpaper, print.
- Vibe: minimalistic, dark, modern.
- Must NOT be a generic tech glyph or something an SVG generator produces.
- Existing DESIGN.md palette to honour: charcoal `#15181A`→`#0F1214`,
  caramel `#C89B6B`, teal `#5B8A8F`, ink `#ECEAE6`.

## Category clichés — off limits
Named from the reference library's developer-tools set (26% circle geometry,
34% containment, 19% negative space, 19% blue):
spiral / brain / neuron · circuit nodes · shield · cloud · upward arrow ·
chat bubble · gear · infinity-lazy-8 · puzzle piece · rocket · magnifier ·
the lettermark "E" in a default geometric sans · a circle with a plain gap
(reads as a loading spinner).

## Rejected in round 1
Three concepts were built, rendered and honestly tested, then thrown out:

| Concept | Formal operation | Why it failed |
|---|---|---|
| The Fold | ring radius × 1.31, folded at a 100° vertex | read as a **map pin / teardrop** at every angle tested (84/96/108°) |
| The Thread | monotone-stroke logarithmic spiral | read as a **generic swirl** — the "e" of a stock tech mark |
| The Keystone | ring segmented into 10 witnessed spans | read as a **loading spinner** |

Kept from that round: the *argument*, not the form.

## The surviving idea
Evo's substance is not a shape — it is **a record that does not break**. So the
mark is a single continuous geometric body that is **not divided but displaced**:
one line is drawn through it, and the half on one side of that line slips along
it. Nothing is added, nothing is lost, the body is still one body.

## The three directions
- **A — The Seam.** A ring, cut by one seam, the upper half slipped along it.
- **B — The Slip.** The same operation as one solid mass; the heavy cut.
- **C — The Thread.** The name itself, drawn as one unbroken monoline stroke.

## Recommendation
**A — The Seam.** It survives 16 px, it is one shape in one colour, it reverses,
it embroideries as a single stitch path, and it is not in the category's
repertoire. Its honest risk is at 16 px, where a fast glance can read it as a
sync/refresh glyph; the seam and the slip are what pull it back out.
