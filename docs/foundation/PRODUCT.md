# Product

> "You should only have to remember what you want to do.
> Everything else should already be there."

---

# Before You Begin

Before you continue reading this document, answer one question.

**When you sit down to continue something important, what do you do first?**

Do you immediately begin working?

Or do you first try to remember?

Where the files were.

Which browser tabs mattered.

What happened in the last meeting.

Why you left that comment.

Which version was the latest.

Where your notes were.

What you had already decided.

What you planned to do next.

Most people don't notice they're doing this.

They've done it so many times that it feels like part of work.

We don't believe it is.

---

# The Invisible Work

Every interruption creates two kinds of work.

The work you intended to do.

And the work required before you can begin doing it again.

The second kind is invisible.

It doesn't appear on your calendar.

It isn't measured.

It creates nothing.

Yet every knowledge worker performs it every single day.

Finding documents.

Opening applications.

Searching conversations.

Reopening browser tabs.

Looking through notes.

Trying to remember where you stopped.

Trying to get back into it.

None of this is your real work.

It is simply the price computers ask you to pay before they allow you to continue.

We believe that price should be zero.

---

# The Promise

You should only have to remember **what** you want to do.

Never **how** to get back to doing it.

You decide the intention.

Evo reconstructs everything required to continue.

If you want to continue your presentation...

say so.

If you want to continue yesterday's meeting...

say so.

If you want to continue your research...

say so.

Everything after that becomes Evo's responsibility.

---

# What Evo Is

Evo is a local-first system that continuously understands the work happening on your computer.

Not so it can replace you.

Not so it can think for you.

But so it can eliminate the effort required to get back into your work.

It quietly learns which resources belong together.

Files.

Repositories.

Browser tabs.

Notes.

Documents.

Conversations.

Meetings.

Research.

Then, whenever you decide to continue something, Evo reconstructs the complete environment that thought belongs in.

Not just the document.

Everything around it.

Because meaningful work rarely lives inside a single application.

---

# The Experience

The perfect interaction with Evo should feel almost impossible.

You open your computer.

You don't search.

You don't organize.

You don't prepare.

You simply say:

> "Hey Evo, continue working on the investor deck."

Or:

> "Hey Evo, what was I doing with Bhavit yesterday?"

Or:

> "Hey Evo, bring back everything related to the hiring plan."

Within seconds...

your work is back.

The document.

The browser tabs.

The notes.

The conversation.

The repository.

The terminal.

The research.

The exact environment your mind expected to find.

It should not feel like software opened.

It should feel like the interruption never happened.

---

# What We Believe

People already know what they want to do.

What slows them down is remembering everything required before they can do it.

Computers remember files.

People remember intentions.

Evo exists to bridge that gap.

Technology should preserve continuity.

Not demand reconstruction.

---

# What Evo Refuses To Become

Evo is not another chatbot.

It is not another note-taking application.

It is not another project manager.

It is not another browser.

It is not another search engine.

It is not another dashboard.

Evo does not ask people to organize their work.

It accepts work exactly as it happens.

The system adapts to people.

People should never have to adapt to the system.

---

# Principles of Interaction

Every interaction should reduce thought, never increase it.

Evo remains invisible until needed.

Evo never interrupts without reason.

Evo never pretends certainty.

Evo always explains its actions honestly.

Evo protects privacy before convenience.

The fastest path back into meaningful work is always the correct one.

---

# Success

Evo succeeds the day someone opens their computer and instinctively says:

> "Hey Evo, continue."

Not because they remembered to use Evo.

But because they forgot there was ever another way to continue their work.

When getting back into work becomes effortless...

Evo has done its job.

Everything else is implementation.
