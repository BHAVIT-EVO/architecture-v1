## 3. Superhuman manual-onboarding economics

| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| Onboarding format | 30-min 1:1 call; user pays **$30/mo before** the call |
| Throughput | 8–12 half-hour calls/day/specialist (4–6 hrs/day) |
| Team size | up to **20 FT specialists** at peak |
| Cost/specialist | **$60K–$130K** fully loaded |
| ARR/specialist | **~$650K** (formula: 40 customers × 45 weeks × $30/mo × 12 months = $648,000) |
| Scale reached | 20,000+ users / $36M ARR; later switched to **14-day self-serve trial** |

**Arithmetic:** 40 × 45 × 30 × 12 = **648,000 ≈ $650K** — the scalar ties out, and is consistent with 8–12 calls/day (≈40–60/week). But the formula is **dimensionally sloppy** ("40 customers" omits "/week"; multiplying customers × weeks × $/mo × months) and assumes zero churn / full-year payment, so it's a gross steady-state figure, not net ARR. This is Superhuman's own First Round Review number, faithfully reproduced.

## 6. Playbook patterns (what repeatedly worked)

1. **Waitlist + staged invites** as a scarcity/quality-control funnel (Superhuman, Linear, Arc, Raycast).
2. **High-touch manual onboarding** at small scale before automating (Superhuman, Granola, Rewind).
3. **Community-first before launch** (Rize ~300 passionate users; Obsidian Discord).
4. **Developer-channel spikes** — Show HN (Warp 10K day one), MIT/YC network (Cursor first 1,000).
5. **YouTube / creator distribution** (Arc, Rize).
6. **PH as an amplifier of an existing audience, not a cold-start** ("amplified what each founder already had").
7. **Long free runway** before monetizing (Raycast ~3yr, Cursor "monk" year).

## 7. What no longer works (2026)

- **PH as a cold launch button** — needs a pre-built 500-follower + 200–300-email audience; treating launch day as a "publish button" fails.
- **Waitlists left to sit** — "atrophy" fast (Rewind, Superhuman); interest ≠ purchase intent.
- **Usage-capping paywalls** on a habit product — backfired for Rewind (switched to unlimited).
- **Paid social/search ads for indie Mac tools** — Rize tested Instagram/Reddit ads with no results.
- **Building another developer-tooling company from scratch** — Zach Lloyd (Warp) wouldn't.
- **r/MacOS promo** — no promo thread allowed since Sept 2025; r/macapps requires mod permission + account age + karma.

## Bottom line for Evo (local-first, no-cloud premise)

The GTM evidence corroborates the pricing digest: the local-first heroes here (**Obsidian, Bear, Warp, Things**) either monetize via a **cloud add-on** (Obsidian Sync/Publish, Bear iCloud sync) or are **one-time purchases** (Things), and Warp explicitly states the local tool "was never the business." No case demonstrates a pure local-only, no-cloud recurring subscription. Realistic first-1,000 motions for a 2-person team with no audience: build a small passionate community first (Rize model), a curated waitlist with staged invites (Linear: ~1 year to convert ~1,000 of a 10,000 list at ~10%), or a developer-channel spike (Show HN, best-documented ~94 trial signups/day; Warp's 10K was a waitlist, not activations). Expect **~3% waitlist→paying pre-launch, ~10% once live**, and PH only amplifies an audience you already have.
