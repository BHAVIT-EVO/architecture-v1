## SECTION 5 — "WHAT THIS MEANS FOR A PRE-SEED RAISE IN THIS SPACE"
*(the report explicitly labels all 8 as "My interpretation (not fact)")*

1. **Your category is fundable but crowded.** "At least 8 directly comparable pre-seed/seed rounds closed in the last 18 months in personal AI, local-first, and AI-desktop tooling. Investors are writing checks, but you're competing against a visible cohort."
2. **Pre-seed caps are compressing slightly, but AI still commands a premium.** "Median pre-seed pre-money valuations dipped from $8.0M to $7.7M (Q4 2024 → Q3 2025), but AI startups capture ~50% of pre-seed dollars. Your 'local-first macOS AI' positioning should resonate, but don't expect a 2x AI premium at pre-seed—that premium shows up at Series A (5x) and later."
3. **Expect to raise $1M–$2.5M on a $10M–$15M post-money SAFE cap.** "Given your SF/NY focus and AI label, **$12M–$15M is a realistic target range**."
4. **Dilution will be ~15–18% for a standard pre-seed.** "Carta's 'golden metric' is 15.5% dilution for pre-seed ($1M+). If you raise $1.5M at a $12M post-money cap, that's 12.5% dilution; at $10M post, it's 15%."
5. **Prosumer is bifurcated—position carefully.** "Frame your product as **'prosumer productivity' or 'developer tooling' rather than 'consumer subscription'** to align with active investors."
6. **Local-first is a differentiator but not a valuation driver.** "Epicenter (local-first, SQLite, plain text) raised only $500K at pre-seed, while cloud-first AI memory plays (Supermemory, $2.6M–$3M) raised more.
